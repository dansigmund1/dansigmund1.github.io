Biography

I was born in September, 2000, in South Boston, MA. After 9 months, my family moved to Winthrop, MA. This is where I made my earliest memories. Once I started attending a private school in Boston, we moved to Charlestown, MA, which I consider to be my hometown. I attended Boston College High School in Dorchester, MA, from grades 7 - 12. Upon graduation, I moved out of state for the first time, down to Philadelphia, PA, to attend Villanova University. After graduating with a Bachelor's of Science in Computer Science, and a minor in Engineering Entrepreneurship, I moved back to Boston to work for Cenotech Solutions. At the moment, I currently reside in South Boston, MA, with the goal of moving to the San Francisco Bay Area by the end of the year.
